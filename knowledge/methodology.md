# Chart reading method

This file is a placeholder decision procedure. A human must replace it with a distilled method before reports are treated as method-grounded. It is not a textbook, not a trading system, and not investment advice. Cite the section headings below. If a judgement is not covered here, say so. Do not invent a method.

## Order of work

Read the longest timeframe first and use it only as context. Read the shortest timeframe last and use it only for timing. Then state agreement or disagreement across the timeframes that were actually captured. Do not treat a missing timeframe as agreement.

## What to read

From each image, describe only visible structure: swing direction, whether price is holding above or below an obvious prior extreme, and wick extremes you can see. Levels read off an axis are approximate. Name the timeframe the image was labelled with.

## What must not be invented

Do not invent indicator values, oscillator readings, moving-average values, or volume figures that are not drawn on the image. Do not infer a numerical price the axis does not show. Do not claim a timeframe that the image label does not match. If an image is blank, obscured, or the wrong timeframe, say so.

## Agreement and invalidation

Confluence is the same visible structure appearing on more than one captured timeframe. A conflict is visible structure that disagrees across timeframes. Invalidation is the visible level or structure break that would make the stated bias no longer match the images. Do not express invalidation as an order to trade.
